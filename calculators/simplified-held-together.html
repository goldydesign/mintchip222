Pick two yarn weights and this estimates the combined yarn weight, without knowing the yardage or grams. It will be less accurate than the above option. (approx).

How this works (quick)

This uses typical WPI (wraps per inch) ranges per yarn weight. When holding two strands together, it estimates the combined thickness by adding cross-sectional area (so thickness doesn't just "add"). It's a solid starting point — always swatch if it matters.
